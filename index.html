The Data Keepers

Who decides how community data is used?

Indigenous Data Sovereignty includes community authority over how data about a community is collected, accessed, used and governed. Individual privacy and consent matter alongside that collective authority.

Warranga and this scenario are fictional. This pilot has no community endorsement and is not an accredited assessment. Community consultation and Indigenous-led review are required before presenting it as a community-endorsed resource.
